## Notes for crawlers

- FAQPage JSON-LD is only emitted where the Q&A is visible on the page (`#faq`): the home page, every hub (patterns, teardowns, guides, glossary, prompts, skills, inspirations) and its category or lane pages, plus pattern, prompt pack, glossary, teardown, comparison, and how-to guide pages, /faq, and /about. Hub search results (`?q=`) hide the FAQ and omit the schema.
- Teardown comparisons (/teardowns/compare/{jobSlug}) open with a Bottom line summary and an At a glance table (dimension × product) before the screenshot sections.
- /inspirations is a CollectionPage with ItemList JSON-LD; each /inspirations/{id} page has CreativeWork schema and an At a glance block.
- Inspirations lanes (/inspirations/shaders, /inspirations/motion, etc.) are CollectionPage routes with filtered ItemList JSON-LD.
- Inspirations detail breadcrumbs include the parent lane (Home → Inspirations → Lane → Project).
- Teardown topic hubs live at /teardowns/{jobSlug}: a CollectionPage with an ItemList of every teardown for that job, linked to its comparison.
- Teardown job comparisons live at /teardowns/compare/{jobSlug} (composer, output, onboarding, human-in-the-loop, citations, personalization, model selection, pricing); product teardowns remain at /teardowns/{product}/{guide}.
- Product teardown guides and glossary terms include visible FAQs with matching FAQPage JSON-LD; glossary terms also emit DefinedTerm schema.
- Prompt packs (/prompts/for-*) emit HowTo JSON-LD from their step lists. Workflows live at /workflows and /workflows/{id}; /playbooks and /playbooks/{id} 301 to the matching workflow URL.
- Gallery (/gallery, /gallery/{id}) is noindex, follow — screenshot assets used by teardowns; each item links to its teardown. Filter URLs (?product=) canonicalize to /gallery.
- Binder how-to guides (`/guides/how-to-design-*`) include a visible FAQ (`#faq`) with FAQPage JSON-LD and HowTo JSON-LD from their ship-next steps.
- Pattern detail pages include live UI demos inline; there is no separate /demos section.
- Pattern topic hub URLs and /frameworks redirect to /patterns; /design-md redirects to /skills/stitch-design-md.
- Canonical host is https://aiuxplayground.com (non-www); URLs are slashless.
- /search?* is noindex.
- Bingbot and msnbot are allowed; IndexNow ownership key is published at site root for faster Bing/ChatGPT discovery after content updates.
